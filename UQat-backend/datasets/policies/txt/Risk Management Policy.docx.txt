1. Purpose
This policy establishes a structured approach to identifying, assessing, mitigating, and
monitoring risks that may impact DanfeCorp's operations, assets, data, and reputation. It
ensures that risk management practices are integrated into all business processes and
decision-making activities.
2. Scope
This policy applies to all DanfeCorp employees, contractors, business units, and
third-party vendors involved in handling organizational assets, data, and operations. It
covers risks related to financial, operational, security, compliance, and reputation.
Effective risk management is essential to DanfeCorp's success and sustainability. By
systematically identifying, assessing, and addressing risks, we can protect our assets,
maintain business continuity, and capitalize on opportunities while minimizing potential
negative impacts. This policy provides the foundation for a consistent approach to risk
management across the organization.

4. Risk Management Framework
DanfeCorp follows a comprehensive risk management framework that includes the
following key activities:
4.1 Risk Identification
● Identify potential risks related to business operations, IT infrastructure, financial
activities, and regulatory compliance.
● Utilize risk registers, security assessments, incident reports, and industry threat
intelligence.
4.2 Risk Assessment
● Assess risks based on Likelihood (Low, Medium, High) and Impact (Low, Medium,
High).
● Employ qualitative and quantitative risk assessment methodologies.
● Assign risk owners responsible for managing and mitigating identified risks.
4.3 Risk Mitigation and Treatment
● Develop and implement risk mitigation plans to reduce the likelihood or impact of
identified risks.
● Apply appropriate risk treatment strategies:
Risk Avoidance: Eliminate activities that introduce unacceptable
risks.Risk Reduction: Implement controls to minimize risks.Risk Transfer: Shift risks
through insurance, outsourcing, or contractual agreements.Risk Acceptance: Accept
risks that fall within DanfeCorp's risk tolerance.
● Risk Avoidance: Eliminate activities that introduce unacceptable risks.
● Risk Reduction: Implement controls to minimize risks.
● Risk Transfer: Shift risks through insurance, outsourcing, or contractual agreements.
● Risk Acceptance: Accept risks that fall within DanfeCorp's risk tolerance.
4.4 Risk Monitoring and Reporting
● Continuously monitor risks and track remediation efforts.
● Conduct periodic risk reviews and update risk registers accordingly.
● Report significant risks to executive leadership and relevant stakeholders.

5. Incident and Crisis Management
Even with robust risk management practices, incidents may still occur. DanfeCorp
maintains comprehensive incident and crisis management procedures to address
high-impact risks when they materialize, ensuring rapid response and minimized
business disruption.
● Establish response plans for high-impact risks.
● Ensure rapid containment, investigation, and mitigation of security incidents.
● Conduct post-incident reviews to capture lessons learned and improve risk controls.
6. Compliance and Regulatory Considerations
● Ensure alignment with applicable regulatory requirements, industry standards, and
best practices.
● Conduct regular audits and assessments to maintain compliance.
● Engage with external auditors and regulatory bodies as necessary.
7. Policy Review and Updates
This policy shall be reviewed and updated annually or in response to significant changes
in DanfeCorp's business operations, regulatory landscape, or risk environment. All
updates must be approved by the Security Team.
This policy document supersedes all previous risk management guidelines and becomes
effective as of the date specified above. All departments must align their operations with
these requirements within 30 days of the effective date.
For questions or clarifications regarding this policy, contact the Risk Management Team
via secure channels only.
